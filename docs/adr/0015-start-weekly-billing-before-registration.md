# Start weekly billing before registration

First-time customers make a real weekly payment through Stripe Checkout before creating a JobTrackr User, and their first paid week follows Stripe's subscription billing period even if they delay registration. At launch, Checkout accepts payment methods that confirm promptly so buyers can proceed to registration after successful payment. Registration fixes the Primary Email to the Checkout Email and lets the buyer choose password signup with a Resend-delivered verification link or Google signup with the same verified email; either path consumes one paid Registration Claim. A buyer who leaves before registering can request a one-time registration recovery link sent to that Checkout Email, avoiding another purchase; the claim expires when paid access ends, so an expired, unregistered buyer must subscribe again. This keeps public signup closed and billing identity unambiguous, at the cost of making post-Checkout registration time-sensitive and requiring transactional email delivery.
