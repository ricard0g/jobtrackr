# Enforce ATS Structure deterministically and Grounded Tailoring via playbook

ATS Structure and presentation (section order, headings, typography, contact layout, DOCX pixel fidelity, and shared structure across Output Formats) are enforced in the Canonical CV schema and renderers with tests—not by hoping the drafting model formats well. Generated CV content follows Grounded Tailoring: draft and revise prompts operationalize `docs/cv-generation/ats-content-rules.md`, and deterministic validation rejects invented employers, skills, duties, dates, and metrics against Candidate Evidence. Job Description analysis remains targeting-only (keywords, themes, values, title cues)—never a source of candidate facts—respecting ADR-0001’s evidence-before-tailoring boundary. Temporary research downloads under `docs/temporary-cv-gen-improvement-docs/` are extraction inputs for the playbook, not the long-term policy store.
