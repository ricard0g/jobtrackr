# Do not link Google identities by email

JobTrackr identifies a returning Google Sign-in Identity by Google's stable `sub` claim and never links it to an existing User from an email match alone. An unknown Google identity with an unused email creates a new User, while a collision requires password reauthentication followed by an explicit link whose Google email exactly matches the User's primary email case-insensitively. Until JobTrackr has its own email-verification flow, a successful Google sign-in or exact-match link may mark that Primary Email verified, but verification status never authorizes automatic linking. This adds friction but prevents a verified yet non-authoritative or reassigned email address from taking over an existing User; an unverified password User is never silently claimed by Google, and adding JobTrackr-owned email verification remains required before broader public rollout.
