# Sell weekly subscription access

JobTrackr will sell €10.99 weekly recurring access, including applicable tax, with unlimited CV Generation funded by JobTrackr and at most 20 saved Generated CVs per Application; the landing states these terms plainly and displays only the EUR price. EUR is the base billing currency, while Stripe Checkout may present a converted USD amount to buyers who choose USD; the landing does not quote or explain the converted amount. Paid Users may start one CV Generation at a time per Application and have several in progress across different Applications. Each buyer may have at most one active JobTrackr Subscription, including before registration, when the Billing Customer and Checkout Email identify the buyer. This replaces one-time, generation-credit, and customer-supplied-key offers because recurring revenue supports ongoing model costs without requiring customers to manage an AI provider account, while excessive use still needs safeguards. Cancelling renewal preserves paid features through the already paid week; a failed payment suspends them immediately and triggers retries on days 1 and 3, with restoration on success and cancellation after both retries fail.
